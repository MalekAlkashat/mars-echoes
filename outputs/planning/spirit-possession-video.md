## Shot

One continuous, approximately ten-second 16:9 shot. Grounded cinematic Mars,
existing Opportunity MER design, current soft blue-cyan spirit. Duration and
resolution must be reconciled with the chosen model's live options before submission.

0–2.5 s: Low rear-three-quarter tracking view. The pale, weathered six-wheel rover
rests motionless on cold orange Mars. The soft cyan soul glides into frame with
a trailing blue wake, slows, and gathers itself beside the rover. Keep the soul
recognizable as a soft luminous presence, without a face, limbs or a hard outline.

2.5–4 s: The spirit makes a small buoyant leap into the centre of the rover's
chassis. Its body flows inward like light entering machinery. The trailing wake
is drawn in last. The independent spirit completely disappears into the rover.
No impact, explosion, portal or new physical attachment.

4–6 s: Hold the third-person framing for a quiet settling beat. A single gentle
cyan pulse spreads from the entry point through existing body seams to the mast.
It settles into a faint blue-cyan halo around the rover and a restrained cool
tint on nearby metal. The camera briefly becomes still. One small mast movement
suggests awareness; retain the real mechanical silhouette. The entire robot
does not become solid blue or sprout a floating halo ring.

6–9 s: Only after the halo settles, smoothly dolly upward and forward toward one
existing mast optical lens. Let cyan light occupy the image briefly as the
camera reaches the lens. Resolve into the rover's outward-looking optical view,
facing the same Mars landscape along a physically consistent axis.

9–10 s: Hold the rover first-person perspective. A faint blue peripheral haze
and one slow settling pulse carry the spirit's colour into the new view. The
image steadies, ready for player control. No human cockpit or driver interior.

## Generation prompt

Create a single continuous cinematic possession shot, approximately ten seconds,
landscape 16:9. Match the supplied references for the actual six-wheel Opportunity
Mars rover, terrain, lighting and soft blue-cyan spirit. A floating, softly
luminous cyan soul approaches the inactive rover, gathers its flowing wake, then
makes one gentle arcing leap into the centre of the chassis. The soul and its
entire trail are visibly absorbed; no independent orb remains outside afterward.
Hold the exterior shot for two seconds after absorption. One soft cyan pulse
spreads through existing body seams to the mast, then settles into a subtle
blue-cyan aura and restrained cool surface tint. The rover makes a tiny awakening
mast movement. Only now move the camera smoothly toward an existing mast lens,
passing into a first-person optical viewpoint looking outward across that same
Mars landscape. Keep a delicate cyan peripheral glow and slow settling pulse in
the final view. The soul's identity transfers through continuous motion, shared
colour and one uninterrupted pulse: the audience must clearly understand that
the spirit now inhabits the rover. Believable weathered machinery, cold orange
Mars, atmospheric distance, soft volumetric-looking light, deliberate restrained
camera movement. Preserve the rover's real proportions, wheel count and optical
mast. No text, HUD, logos, subtitles, human cockpit, human driver, cartoon eyes,
explosions, hard-edged magic ring, strobe, teleport cut or equipment redesign.
