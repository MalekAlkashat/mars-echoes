# Repair interaction references

Research date: 11 November 2026. These are developer references, not selected art or instructions to copy a game.

- [Fireproof Games: development approach](https://www.fireproofgames.com/about). Their account of The Room connects camera design with tactile object examination. Application to this prototype: keep module, keyed socket and manipulation in one stable external close-up.
- [Thomas Grip: Let's not forget about Physics](https://frictionalgames.com/2010-09-lets-not-forget-about-physics/). Frictional describes making object placement precise, mapping 2D input into 3D manipulation, and removing unwanted lag and bounce. Application: constrain the cartridge to two position axes and one rotation axis, with immediate input response.
- [Frictional: Level scripting best practices](https://wiki.frictionalgames.com/page/HPL3/Scripting/Level_Scripting_-_Best_Practices). The guide favors narrative continuity and recoverable challenges. Application: misalignment meets a mechanical stop, with adjustment available immediately and no restart penalty.

These applications are implementation inferences. The user has explicitly chosen WASD driving and a cinematic visual style. Camera, art and story remain governed by the project's selected references.
