# Mission 2 source and usage record

Acquired 14 November 2026 for Opportunity's April–June 2005 Purgatory Dune chapter. Item-level source URLs, credits, capture dates/sols, transformations and cryptographic hashes are in [manifest.json](manifest.json) and [preview-manifest.json](preview-manifest.json).

## NASA observations and engineering photograph

PIA07216: **NASA/JPL/Cornell**. PIA07999, PIA07922 and its Figure 1, PIA07895, PIA07983/84/85/86: **NASA/JPL** as credited on their Photojournal pages. All acquired image and GIF originals are unchanged. No NASA logo or seal was downloaded as an asset. The record uses the Photojournal credit; alternative migrated resource pages may style it NASA/JPL-Caltech.

These are NASA media resources, not assets labeled CC0. Follow [NASA images and media guidance](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/), including credit, third-party exceptions, trademarks and the prohibition on implied endorsement. A non-executable text extraction of that guidance is retained under `sources/nasa-media-guidance.txt`. The project's overall license does not replace individual asset terms.

NASA caption text was downloaded and extracted without navigation/scripts for offline research. `sourceHtmlSha256` identifies the retrieved HTML bytes; `sha256` identifies the derived text file. Dynamic navigation/HTML can change between future acquisitions. The NASA pages remain the canonical source. Preview images are labeled derivatives: RGB conversion, proportional downsampling and JPEG compression; the GIF poster is frame 0. Neither previews nor supplied diagrams are measured terrain data.

## Poly Haven material candidate

[Dense Sand](https://polyhaven.com/a/dense_sand), **Dimitrios Savva / Poly Haven**. Four original 2048 × 2048 JPGs: diffuse, OpenGL normal, roughness and ambient occlusion. [Poly Haven asset license: CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license). Credit is retained for project provenance even though CC0 does not require attribution. This dedication covers the asset maps, not all website text, logos or example renders.

The official file and asset metadata are retained under `sources/dense-sand-files.json` and `sources/dense-sand-info.json`. All four maps match the supplier's byte counts and MD5s as well as the pack's SHA256 records. The scan is a terrestrial analogue and remains an unapproved runtime candidate; NASA observations govern final morphology, palette and scale. Maps have not been recolored, tiled, repainted or used to modify existing game assets.
