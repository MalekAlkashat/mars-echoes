## Rights

- [NASA media guidance](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/) describes educational/informational use and attribution, with separate restrictions for marks, endorsement, identifiable people and third-party protected material. NASA hosting alone does not establish CC0, ownership of every contribution or an unrestricted license for every use.
- No item selected here is labeled CC0. Retain its full credit and its actual caption/rights record when preparing runtime derivatives.
- [HiRISE ESP_056955_1775, Usage Policy](https://www.uahirise.org/ESP_056955_1775) explicitly states that images produced by HiRISE on that site are public domain and requests NASA/JPL-Caltech/UArizona credit. This item-specific statement applies to R07; R06 retains its published spelling of the credit.
- [NTRS 20190027303](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20190027303) identifies the paper as a US Government work with public use permitted. Keep author, title and meeting attribution.
- Partner names identify contributors; no independent blanket grant is inferred for arbitrary other material from those organizations.
- Retained source HTML may contain site navigation, marks and unrelated images/links. It is offline research evidence, not material to ship into the game.
- Avoid endorsement implications and decorative NASA logos. No person-focused promotional image or additional music/audio license was acquired.

## Transformations

Original image/PDF bytes are unchanged after acquisition. previews/M3-R01.jpg through M3-R09.jpg use aspect-preserving downsampling, RGB conversion and JPEG encoding for browsing. Small originals were not upscaled. previews/contact-sheet.jpg assembles those references with clear identifiers/dimensions; it is a catalog artifact, not an archival photograph.

No cropping, recoloring, denoising, inpainting, generative completion or new historically styled image was performed. Neither apparent grain nor missing portions were “repaired.” The catalog retains publisher originals through local links. Preview hashes and transformations appear under manifest.derivatives.

Sources/Sxx.html retain fetched page bytes; Sxx.txt is an HTMLParser-derived reading aid and may include navigation. Cite original headings/captions or paper page numbers, not generated text-line positions. The five web-read pages without valid direct downloads remain link-only; source-notes.md records the limitation.
