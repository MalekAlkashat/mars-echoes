ORIGINAL 3D RECONSTRUCTION MANNEQUINS

Four faceless neutral mannequins: standing, walking pose, crouching, and reaching.
These are original static 3D pose proxies, not rigged or animated character actors.
They are suitable for holographic reconstruction snapshots and position markers.

Format: self-contained GLB 2.0. Units: meters. +Y up. +Z forward.
Two transparent emissive unlit materials: cyan volume and thin cyan contour geometry.
No textures, environments, background meshes or external dependencies are required.
The narrow oval at ground level is a placement locator and can be hidden.
You can replace the mannequin with your own rigged character and use the UI kit over it.

Transparency and additive bloom are renderer-dependent. In an engine, use an unlit
transparent material, disable shadow casting, and optionally add a gentle bloom.
